FAQs

Common questions

What types of projects do you take on?{{icon:plus}}

Commercial, industrial, institutional, government and residential buildings, from a single set of sliding doors to the full curtain wall of a building.

Do you manufacture your own products?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. We fabricate aluminum and glass components in our plant in Las Palmas Industrial Park, Cataño, and install them on site.

Do you work on projects outside Puerto Rico?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. We take on international projects too. Send us the details and a project manager will get in touch.

How do I request a proposal?{{icon:plus}}

Use the proposal form, email {{site.email}} or call {{site.phone}}. Plans, specifications or photos help us respond faster.

Do you work with government agencies?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. We’re a registered federal contractor and have completed work on federal buildings. See our About page for our NAICS codes.
