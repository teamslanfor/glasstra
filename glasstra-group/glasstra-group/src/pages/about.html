About Glasstra Group

Quality and innovation in architectural glass and aluminum, in Puerto Rico since 1978.

Our story

Over four decades of glass and glazing in Puerto Rico

Glasstra has worked in Puerto Rico as a glass and glazing contractor since 1978. Over that time, our reputation for the quality of our products and our work on complicated projects has grown.

We offer architectural glass and aluminum solutions, along with doors and windows for homes, offices and commercial spaces. Our work covers everything from developing the concept to manufacturing every component of a project, and we install what we build.

Every project is unique, but they all share one thing: our focus on quality and innovation.
